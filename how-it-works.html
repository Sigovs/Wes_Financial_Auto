The process

Five steps
to completion.

  1. 01Submit your vehicle
  2. 02Evaluation
  3. 03Your offer
  4. 04Documentation & payment
  5. 05Collection
  1. 01

    Submit your vehicle

    Share the essential details of your vehicle through our online form, by phone or by email. A complete submission allows our team to provide an accurate assessment from the outset.

    • Year, make, model and mileage
    • Specification and optional equipment
    • Recent photographs, interior and exterior
    • Service history and any known condition notes
  2. 02

    Evaluation

    Our specialists review every submission individually. Each vehicle is assessed against current market conditions, comparable transactions, condition, specification and provenance.

    • Market position and current demand
    • Condition, specification and originality
    • Ownership and service documentation
  3. 03

    Your offer

    We present a clear, written offer together with the reasoning behind it. Our offers carry no obligation, giving you the time and information to make a fully informed decision.

    • A transparent, market-based valuation
    • Direct sale and consignment options where appropriate
    • A dedicated point of contact for any questions
  4. 04

    Documentation & payment

    Once you accept, our team prepares and manages all transaction documentation. Where a loan is outstanding, we coordinate the payoff directly with your lender and confirm every step in writing.

    • Bill of sale and title transfer
    • Lender payoff and lien release
    • Secure, verified payment
  5. 05

    Collection

    We arrange the collection of your vehicle at a time and location that suits you, with a final inspection and handover completed by our team. The transaction closes with full confirmation in writing.

    • Scheduled at your convenience
    • Final inspection and key handover
    • Written confirmation of completion

Selling options

Two ways to sell

Direct sale

We purchase your vehicle outright. A single, transparent offer and a fully managed transaction — the most efficient route to completion.

  • One offer, no obligation
  • Payment on completion
  • Best suited to a prompt, certain sale

Consignment

We market and sell your vehicle on your behalf to our network of qualified buyers, managing presentation, enquiries and the transaction throughout.

  • Professional presentation and marketing
  • Agreed terms before listing
  • Best suited to rare and collectible vehicles

Questions

Frequently
asked.

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Contact our team.

Is there any obligation once I receive an offer?

No. Every offer is provided without obligation. You are free to accept, decline or take time to consider it.

Can I sell a vehicle that still has a loan on it?

Yes. We coordinate the payoff directly with your lender as part of the transaction and confirm the lien release in writing.

What types of vehicles do you acquire?

We specialize in premium, luxury, exotic and collectible vehicles. If you are unsure whether your vehicle qualifies, please contact our team.

How is the value of my vehicle determined?

Our specialists assess current market conditions, comparable transactions, condition, specification, mileage and documentation. The reasoning is shared with you alongside the offer.

What is the difference between a direct sale and consignment?

In a direct sale, we purchase the vehicle outright. With consignment, we market and sell the vehicle on your behalf under terms agreed in advance.

Do I need to bring the vehicle to you?

Not necessarily. Collection can be arranged at a time and location that suits you.
