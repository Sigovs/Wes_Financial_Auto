Who we are

A trusted partner
for highline vehicles.

Exotics of Detroit specializes in the acquisition of premium, luxury and exotic vehicles. Our business is built on a simple principle: every client deserves a clear process, a fair assessment and a decision they can rely on.

Each vehicle is evaluated by our team against current market conditions, condition, specification and service history. We present our offer with full transparency and without obligation, so our clients can make informed decisions with confidence.

From the first enquiry to the final transfer of ownership, we manage every stage of the transaction — documentation, lender payoff and vehicle collection — to the highest professional standard.
